Riders

Every rider in the dataset, across both series. A rider is one identity, not one plate number: changing numbers between seasons does not split a career, and a 250SX coast still belongs to the same person who raced Motocross that summer.

These are counts, not ratings. Wins and podiums are finishing positions in motos; a moto a rider did not start is not counted at all. Ratings live on the Ratings page.
