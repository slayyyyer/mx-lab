How this is built

The dataset records what the timing sheets say. Where a sheet is internally odd — a lap time of 29:24 on a 30-minute moto, a “best lap” that does not appear in the lap list, a final lap printed as a summary — the value is kept and the anomaly is recorded as a warning. Nothing is smoothed, imputed, or filled in from another source.

Rebuilds are byte-identical: keys are sorted, floats are rounded, and no timestamps are stored. The gate separates parser errors (which fail the build) from source quirks (which are reported).
