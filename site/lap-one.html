6.3 — What lap 1 is worth

Rank the riders in a moto by their opening lap, rank them by where they finished, and compare the two orderings. A correlation near 1 means the first lap already wrote the result; near 0 means it told you almost nothing.

By season

Bars are the median correlation across the season's measured motos. Coverage matters as much as the number. 2020 has no lap-1 times at all — the lap sheet prints --.--- for every rider — and 2021–2024 have one for part of the field, so those seasons describe the riders who got a clean start rather than the whole field.
