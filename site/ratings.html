6.6 · Ratings

A rating per rider, by class.

Two ratings per class, updated after every moto. Within a moto every finisher is compared with every other finisher, so beating a deep field moves a rating more than beating a thin one — which is what makes riders from different seasons comparable at all. Elo is the headline number; Glicko-2 carries a rating deviation, which says how settled the number is.

A rating is relative to the riders it met. It is not a prediction about a rider in a different class, and it is not a statement about machinery, fitness or luck.

Elo with K = 32 spread across the field (K ÷ (n − 1) per pairing), starting at 1500; a rider new to a class enters at that class's current median so the scale does not drift. One rating per class runs across all seven seasons, so a number change does not restart it. Glicko-2 uses the same pairwise comparisons with an initial deviation of 350 and tau 0.5; its deviation is shown, not hidden. Ratings are computed from moto finishing positions; riders who did not start a moto are not compared. Dataset and code: github.com/slayyyyer/mx-lab.
