6.6 — Ratings

An Elo rating per class, updated after every moto. Within a moto every finisher is compared with every other finisher, so beating a deep field moves a rating more than beating a thin one — which is what makes riders from different seasons comparable at all.

A rating is relative to the riders it met. It is not a prediction about a rider in a different class, and it is not a statement about machinery, fitness or luck.

Unofficial. Not affiliated with or endorsed by AMA Pro Racing, MX Sports, Pro Motocross, any series, team or rider. Series names appear in plain text only; no logos or marks are used.

Elo with K = 32 spread across the field (K ÷ (n − 1) per pairing), starting at 1500; a rider new to a class enters at that class's current median so the scale does not drift. One rating per class runs across all seven seasons, so a number change does not restart it. Ratings are computed from moto finishing positions; riders who did not start a moto are not compared. Dataset and code: github.com/slayyyyer/mx-lab.
