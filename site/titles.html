6.5 · Titles

The championship, computed.

Season standings scored from finishers, using the standard moto points table. Both motos count, a DNF scores nothing, and the chart tracks the points race round by round. Classes follow the season: Supercross 250 titles are scored per coast, and a showdown does not count as one.

These points are computed. The timing sheets publish finishing positions, not championship points, so this page applies the standard table — 25-22-20-18-16, then one fewer per place to 20th — to every moto. A standings total from a series website may differ where a tie-break or a penalty applied.

Unofficial. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by AMA Pro Racing, MX Sports, Pro Motocross, or any series, team or rider. Series names appear in plain text only; no logos or marks are used. Results are attributed to their timing and scoring providers: the AMA results archive (americanmotocrossresults.com) for 2020–2024 and the LiveTime results sites (results.promotocross.com for Motocross and results.supercrosslive.com for Supercross) for 2025–2026.

Points are computed from the committed results using the standard AMA Pro Motocross moto table (1st 25, 2nd 22, 3rd 20, 4th 18, 5th 16, then one fewer per place to 20th). Ties are broken by countback — most wins, then most seconds, and so on — then rider id. The clinch round is the first round after which no rival can reach the leader's total, counting what the class can still score per round: 50 for a two-moto Motocross round, 25 for a Supercross main. A Supercross Triple Crown round prints the official award on its overall sheet, and that value is used instead of the table for those rounds. Dataset and code: github.com/slayyyyer/mx-lab.
