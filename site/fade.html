6.2 · Fade

Who holds pace, who fades.

How much slower a rider is in the second half of a moto than in the first. A bigger number means the pace fell away; a number near zero means it held.

By track

Median fade across every measured rider in the class's two motos. Tracks that ask more of a rider late in a moto sit at the top.

Who holds pace

Riders with at least ten measured motos in the selected season, lowest median fade first. Two decimals of a percent is the resolution of a stopwatch, not of a rider's fitness — read the spread, not the last digit.

Fade is the percent change in a rider's median lap time between the first and second half of a moto, using laps 2 to the end. Laps slower than 1.4× that rider's own median are dropped as stoppages, and a moto needs six usable laps either side of the split. Lap times come from the AMA results archive (2020–2024) and the LiveTime results site (2025–2026). No lap time is estimated. Dataset and code: github.com/slayyyyer/mx-lab.
