6.1 · Lap chart

Where everyone actually was.

Where each rider was on every lap of a moto. A line that rises is a rider losing places; a line that falls is a rider moving forward.

Both series from 2020. LiveTime prints a position on every lap row for 2025–2026. For 2020–2024 the position comes from the moto's LAP CHART sheet, which prints plates in position order for every lap and is inverted into the same shape. Triple Crown rounds publish three separate mains and no single chart, so those rounds are not charted and no position is estimated.

Per-lap positions come from the timing system's lap table for 2025–2026 ( results.promotocross.com) and from the inverted LAP CHART sheet for 2020–2024, both series (americanmotocrossresults.com). The chart keeps the top twenty finishers of each moto; a line breaks where the source prints no position for that lap. Dataset and code: github.com/slayyyyer/mx-lab.
