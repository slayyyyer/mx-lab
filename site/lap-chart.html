6.1 — The lap chart

Where each rider was on every lap of a moto. A line that rises is a rider losing places; a line that falls is a rider moving forward.

2025–2026 only. Those seasons print a position on every lap. The 2020–2024 sheets print lap times and no position, and none is derived here — a reconstructed position would look authoritative and be wrong. The other analyses on this site cover all seven seasons.
