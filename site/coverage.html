Known gaps

  • 2023 Supercross has 16 rounds, not 17. The PDF archive publishes nothing at S2310; the season table records 16 and no round is invented.
  • No per-lap positions for pre-2025 Motocross. The archive's Supercross rounds publish a LAP CHART sheet, which the dataset inverts into positions; Motocross rounds publish none, so those motos carry lap times and no position. No position is estimated.
  • Triple Crown rounds have no lap chart. Their three separate mains publish no single LAP CHART sheet, and their overall classification has no lap times either, so eleven Supercross rounds across 2020–2024 carry results and no per-lap data.
  • 2020 Motocross prints no lap-1 time at all — --.--- for the whole field. The 2020 Supercross rounds do print it, so the lap-1 analysis is not empty in 2020, only in Motocross.
  • Supercross Triple Crown rounds carry no lap times. Their classification comes from an overall report whose points column is the Olympic sum of places, not a championship award.
  • 2026 Anaheim 1 has no 250 qualifying report, so that round's 250 class commits with an empty qualifying list.
  • Source warnings are kept. Missing final laps, red-flag lap times and printed best laps that are not in the lap list are recorded with the value as printed.
