6.4 · Qualifying

What qualifying is worth.

Qualifying sets the gate, and the gate is supposed to matter. This compares where a rider qualified with where they actually finished, across both motos of the same event.

The qualifying sheet lists the whole entry list — around 70 riders for a 40-rider moto field. Riders who did not make the motos have no race result, so they are not counted; only riders who appear in both sessions are compared.

Positions gained

Qualifying position minus mean finishing position. Positive means the rider moved forward when it counted; riders need at least three paired events to appear.

If the gate were the grid

Re-ranking the field by qualifying order and comparing it with the finishing order. “Median move” is the median absolute number of places between the two; “pole win rate” is how often the fastest qualifier actually won.

Qualifying times come from the combined qualifying sheet each class publishes, and finishing positions from the moto results. Positions gained is qualifying position minus the mean of the rider's moto finishes at that event. Correlation is Spearman rho between qualifying position and mean moto finish; re-ranking compares the same two orders by absolute places moved and by how often the fastest qualifier won.
